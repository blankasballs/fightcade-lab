About Fightcade Lab

Why I built this

Fighting games are one of my favorite ways to learn. Every round gives immediate feedback: try an idea, see what breaks, adjust, and run it back. The frustrating part has always been the gap between spotting a problem in a replay and actually practicing an answer. Fightcade Lab is meant to close that gap. Scrub a set with hitboxes, take a note, jump into Replay Takeover at that exact frame, and share short clips with training partners.

Free as in beer, free as in speech

Fightcade Lab is completely free and open source under AGPL-3.0. There are no subscriptions, no paywalls, no ads, and no plans to monetize or profit from it. You can read the code, fork it, self-host it, or run everything locally on your own PC without ever creating an account.

Local-first and respectful of Fightcade

Your replays, notes, and savestates stay on your machine. Cloud sharing on fightcadelab.com is optional and only stores short clips you choose to share, which you can revoke at any time. The companion never touches live netplay, never injects into running matches, and does not bundle ROMs.

Terms, Privacy & DMCA Policy

No ROMs Hosted: Fightcade Lab never hosts or distributes copyrighted arcade ROMs or emulator binaries. Shared clips are short user-captured study clips (6–15s).

Privacy & Portability: Moments and playlists are private by default (with optional unlisted share links). Signed-in users can export all data as JSON or delete their account and clips anytime.

Takedowns / DMCA: Use the quiet Report button on any shared link or email dmca@fightcadelab.com with the exact link for prompt removal.

License: Fightcade Lab is open-source under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
