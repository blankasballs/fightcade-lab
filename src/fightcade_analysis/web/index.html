Terms, Privacy & DMCA Policy

No ROMs Hosted: Fightcade Lab never hosts or distributes copyrighted arcade ROMs or emulator binaries. Shared clips are short user-captured study clips (6–15s).

Privacy & Portability: Moments and playlists are private by default (with optional unlisted share links). Signed-in users can export all data as JSON or delete their account and clips anytime.
